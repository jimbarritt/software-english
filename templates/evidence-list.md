# Evidence List structure: a local reference cache for Software English

## Canonical sources

- Cochrane Handbook, Chapter 14, "Completing 'Summary of findings' tables and grading the certainty of the evidence": https://www.cochrane.org/authors/handbooks-and-manuals/handbook/current/chapter-14

Cochrane and GRADE jointly define one table structure for evidence
used in systematic reviews. This is a strong, singular standard.
PRISMA 2020 governs reporting of the review process itself (a diagram
of the review steps and a checklist), not the evidence-table format.
The evidence-table structure below belongs to Cochrane and GRADE, not
to PRISMA.

## Required structure ("Summary of findings" table)

Per outcome, the table states:

- A header giving the population, setting, intervention, and
  comparison.
- Up to seven outcomes, ranked by importance.
- The assumed risk and the corresponding risk, each with a 95%
  confidence interval.
- The relative effect.
- The number of participants and the number of studies.
- A GRADE certainty rating.
- A comment and an explanatory footnote, where one applies.

## Common variants

- Evidence profile: a fuller working version of the same table, with
  a full justification for each GRADE rating. Cochrane states a
  guideline developer uses an evidence profile; a Cochrane review
  rarely publishes one directly.
